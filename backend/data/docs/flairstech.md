# FlairsTech – Company Overview

## About the Company

**FlairsTech** is a global AI-powered Managed Services Provider (MSP) headquartered in **Montreal, Canada**. The company has over **1,300 employees** across **five offices** in North America, Europe, and Africa.

### Locations

- **Egypt**
- **Canada** (Montreal – headquarters)
- **Poland**

### Scale & Reach

- Serves **over 2,500 organizations** worldwide
- Network of **over 100 partners** globally
- **95% of clients** return with new projects
- **24/7** availability across regions and time zones
- **Multilingual** services: English, French, Polish, Italian, German, Arabic
- **GDPR compliant** and **ISO-certified** processes with agile delivery

## Product: AIMY QA

**AIMY QA** (AI MY Quality Assurance) is FlairsTech’s AI-powered quality monitoring tool, announced by **CEO Rami Fahim**. It:

- Analyzes interactions across **phone calls, email, chats, and tickets**
- Delivers **data-driven insights** for contact center and technical support teams
- Improves **operational efficiency** while maintaining **data security**
